## Little Introduction

**hi, there. i hope you dont mind sparing a little time to read this before you decide to follow me :)**

you can call me **agam**, **biru**, **akasa**, or anything else that feels like it belongs. a nickname you happen to invent is more than welcome too. all three work just fine, and im not particularly picky about what you choose to call me. i go by **any pronouns**, so use whichever feels most natural when speaking to me. also, im already of **legal age**, somewhere in my late teens.

if personality types and zodiac signs are anything to go by, apparently im an **infp** and a **cancer**, which probably explains at least some of the overthinking, sentimentality, and tendency to get attached to things that probably dont deserve quite that much emotional investment.

i tend to notice little things, keep thoughts around for longer than i probably should, and occasionally find meaning in places where there might not be any. i can be quiet when i first meet someone, but give me enough time and theres a good chance youll eventually have to tell me to stop rambling.

this place is mostly a small collection of whatever happens to catch my eye. movies i cant stop thinking about, books im slowly making my way through, music that finds me at the right hour, half-formed thoughts, and occasionally complete nonsense. 

i like writing as well, so every now and then, some words of mine might find their way here, mingling with the daily ramblings, stray thoughts, and occasional shitposting.
